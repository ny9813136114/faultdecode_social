Samsung Washing Machine 1E error ka matlab: Water level sensor error. Samsung Washing Machine pe 1E code aaye to kya karein, Hindi mein.

1E या 1C का मतलब है पानी का लेवल बताने वाला सेंसर ठीक से काम नहीं कर रहा।

Samsung says 1E or 1C is a water level sensor issue. Samsung UK says to switch off at the mains, wait 1 minute and start a new programme. Samsung US also checks for leaks and excess suds before requesting service.
