LG Washing Machine dE error ka matlab: Door or lid not locking. LG Washing Machine pe dE code aaye to kya karein, Hindi mein.

dE का मतलब है दरवाज़ा या ढक्कन लॉक नहीं हो रहा। अक्सर कोई कपड़ा रबर में फंसा होता है या मशीन बहुत भरी होती है।

dE is LG's door error: the door or lid could not lock. dE1 means the door is not closed properly. dE2 means it is closed but not locked, often from overloading or a cloth caught in the door seal. LG says a reset usually clears it.
