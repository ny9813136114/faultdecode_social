LG Washing Machine PE error ka matlab: Water level sensor error. LG Washing Machine pe PE code aaye to kya karein, Hindi mein.

PE का मतलब है पानी का लेवल बताने वाला सेंसर ठीक से काम नहीं कर रहा। कई बार मशीन दोबारा चालू करने से ठीक हो जाता है।

PE is LG's pressure error: the water level sensor is not working properly. A restart often clears it. On top load models LG also links it to too much foam from extra detergent. If PE keeps coming back, the sensor needs a technician.
